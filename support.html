What the app is

QuantAi By TGC is the read-only companion to the Top Gain trading software, which you use with your own broker accounts. It shows your accounts' MTM today and the final figure of every trading day from our RMS ledger, and draws a Live Activity and widgets. It cannot place, change or cancel an order, and it cannot move money.

Add your phone

There is no sign-up in the app. We map your broker accounts to you and send you a one-time invite. A phone is added once.

  1. Install QuantAi By TGC from the App Store.
  2. Open the invite link we sent you on the iPhone; the app opens at Add this phone. The invite works once and expires after 24 hours. Add your phone between 09:00 and 15:45 IST on a weekday; an invite that arrives in the evening still works the next morning.
    • If the invite is shown as a picture on a computer: open the app, go to Add this phone and point the camera at it, or tap Type the code instead.
    • If you copied the link: open the app and use the Paste button in the card "Got the invite as a link?".
  3. Check the address of the platform that the app shows, give the phone a name, and confirm. The app makes two keys inside the iPhone and shows that the phone is added.
  4. From then on, Face ID (or your passcode) opens the app.

Want to look first? On the first screen, Try the demo shows the app with a sample client's figures. Nothing is real and nothing is sent.

Remove a phone, or a lost phone

  • In the app: More, then Remove this phone. It deletes the phone's keys and everything the app stored, so this phone cannot sign in again.
  • Lost phone: write to us first (below) and we remove the phone from our list at once; it stops working the next time it asks. Then use Find My on the phone.
  • Ask us: write to sujalmodi@gmail.com from the address on your account.

What the messages mean

"The desk is closed."

The app reads from our servers during market hours on weekdays (Indian time), and reads the day's ledger in the evening up to 23:00. Outside those times it asks for nothing, shows the last figures with the time they were taken, and tells you when it reads again. If the server is off for the night, the final figure comes with the next ledger, that night or the next morning, and you get a notification when it is ready.

"Desk not answering. It may be a market holiday."

The desk should be open but the platform has not answered for ten minutes. On a market holiday this is normal. Otherwise try again later, or write to us.

"STALE"

The platform has stopped answering, and the figures on screen are the last known ones, with the time they were taken. Do not act on figures marked STALE without checking your broker.

"This phone cannot reach the box."

The phone has no connection to the platform. Check your internet connection and try again.

"This phone was revoked or is no longer enrolled."

The phone was removed, or its sign-in is no longer accepted. Tap Enrol again and add the phone with a new one-time code.

"This phone's keys do not match. Enrol again."

The keys on the phone are not the ones the platform knows, for example after the phone was restored from a backup. Add the phone again with a new code.

"The owner locked phone access." or "Phone access is switched off on the box."

The platform's owner has paused phone access. Nothing is wrong with your phone. Write to us if it lasts.

Live Activity and widgets

  • Switch on Live Activities and "More frequent updates" for the app in iOS Settings.
  • Add the widgets from the Home Screen or the Lock Screen as for any app.
  • To hide the MTM figure on the locked phone, turn off "Show MTM on the locked phone" in the app's Tools, then Settings, then Privacy.
