2. What the app is

The app is the read-only companion to the Top Gain trading software, for people who use the software with their own broker accounts. It shows your accounts' MTM today and the final figure of every trading day from our RMS ledger, and draws a Live Activity and widgets. It cannot place, change or cancel an order and it cannot move money.

There is no sign-up in the app. A phone is added with a one-time invite that we make for you after mapping your broker accounts to you. The invite works once and expires after 24 hours.

4. What we show you

The figures on screen are your own: the MTM of the accounts mapped to you and their final daily figures from our RMS ledger (net, gross, charges), as the software has them from the broker accounts you connected. These figures come from our server to your phone. The app does not send them back.

5. Why we use it

Only to run the app and the platform: to know which phones are allowed in, to sign them in, to send alerts and lock-screen updates, to check that those updates arrive, and to keep the platform secure. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising or marketing, and we do not follow you across other apps or websites.

6. What stays on your phone

  • The last figures, kept in the app's private storage so that a tab opens with figures at once. iOS file protection applies (readable after the first unlock). They are deleted when you sign out, when the phone is refused by the platform, or when you delete the app.
  • Totals for widgets and the Live Activity, in storage shared only by the app and its widgets. They hold totals and counts, not account names. A setting, "Show MTM on the locked phone", hides the figure there.
  • Settings and a record of this phone: the look, the lock time, the privacy switches, and this phone's name, id, key fingerprints, platform address and the time it was added.
  • The private keys are in the Secure Enclave. Face ID is done by iOS; the app is told only yes or no and never receives face data. If Face ID does not read, the phone passcode is used.
  • The camera is used only to read the one-time code on your computer screen. Nothing is recorded or kept.

7. Where we keep data, and who else handles it

On the platform's servers, in India (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai region), with our own office server in Mumbai as a backup, reached over HTTPS. Apple delivers push notifications and Live Activity updates through its Apple Push Notification service, so Apple handles the push token and the notification in transit under its own terms. Our hosting provider and the service in front of our servers handle data for us as service providers. We disclose data to others only where the law requires it, for example under a lawful order.

8. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you sign out, remove the phone or delete the app.
  • The record of your phone on our side (its name, public keys, id, which account added it, and the times it was added, last used and removed): while the phone is added. When you remove a phone it stops working the next time it asks. The record of a removed phone is marked as removed so it can never be used again. We delete it on request, unless we must keep it for security or legal reasons.
  • Push tokens: deleted as soon as the phone is removed.
  • Delivery reports: kept while the phone is added, and deleted together with its push tokens when the phone is removed.
  • Request logs: kept for security and troubleshooting, and deleted when no longer needed for that purpose.
  • The security log of adds, sign-ins and removals holds device ids and events. It never holds keys, cookies, one-time codes or push tokens. Kept as long as needed to keep accounts secure and to investigate problems; deleted on request where the law allows.

9. Removing your phone, and your choices

  • In the app: More, then Remove this phone. It deletes the phone's keys and everything the app stored, so the phone cannot sign in again.
  • Ask us: write to sujalmodi@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will remove it within 7 working days.
  • On the phone: turn off notifications and Live Activities for the app in iOS Settings; hide the MTM figure on the locked phone and the account names inside the app in the app's Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes what the app stored on the phone. It does not remove the phone from our list: remove it as above.
